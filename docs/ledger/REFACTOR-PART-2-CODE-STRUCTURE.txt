   (b) FINDINGS.

   F1  docs/assets/img/ — FIX
       current: docs/assets/img/{01-landing,02-sourced-answer,
         03-sources-citations}.png
       belongs: beside its only consumer, the repository-root README.md
       rule: the project's own co-location pattern — an asset lives with
         what it describes (backend/src/main/resources/corpus/README.md,
         docker/keycloak/themes/README.md)
       evidence: `git grep -l 01-landing` returns README.md and nothing
         else, for all three images. The docs build never copies
         docs/assets: build_theme_assets() writes $OUTPUT_DIR/assets from
         docs/_theme/css and docs/_theme/js only. So docs/assets/ is a
         source tree that reaches no page of the site it sits inside.
       WHY IT IS MORE THAN TIDINESS: the built site serves /assets/, and
         a reader will reasonably assume docs/assets/ is where it comes
         from. It is not. The next person adding a site image has a trap
         waiting. Cost of the fix: three files moved, three <img src> in
         README.md.
       FIXED 2026-08-24, #89: moved to assets/img/, README.md updated;
         docs/assets/ no longer exists.

   F2  docs/ top-level .md casing — RULING NEEDED
       current: 2 of 7 SCREAMING-KEBAB (DOMAIN-MODEL.md,
         REQUIREMENTS.md), 5 lowercase-kebab (frontend-architecture,
         llm-approach, overview, overview-de, refactor-standards)
       counted: `git ls-files docs/ | awk -F/ 'NF==2 && /\.md$/'`
       the other half of the pattern: all three .txt ledgers are
         SCREAMING (DECISIONS, PROJECT-PHASES, REFACTOR-STANDARDS), 3 of
         3 — so ".txt ledger = SCREAMING" is consistent and only the .md
         pages are split.
       THE CONFLICT: no naming convention is written down anywhere
         (`grep -rniE "file naming|naming convention|kebab"` over
         docs/*.txt and docs/*.md returns nothing), so two readings are
         both available. Either the two are accidental outliers and
         should be domain-model.md and requirements.md; or the casing
         MEANS something — DOMAIN-MODEL and REQUIREMENTS are the two
         normative specification pages and the lowercase five are
         explanatory prose, which would make the split a convention
         nobody wrote down rather than a defect.
       COST IF RENAMED, measured so the ruling is made with it in view:
         DOMAIN-MODEL is cited in 22 tracked files including Java javadoc
         and two SQL migrations; REQUIREMENTS in 10 including
         nav-docs.html. Both are published, so both URLs change.
       WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT: a one-line convention in
         REFACTOR-STANDARDS.txt's DOCS section, whichever way it is
         ruled. The absence of the rule is the finding.
       RULED 2026-08-24, ADR-010, #90: lowercase-kebab for explanatory
         pages, SCREAMING kept for ledgers, ADRs and ADR-TEMPLATE;
         DOMAIN-MODEL.md and REQUIREMENTS.md rename in a follow-up, and
         the three migration comments citing them stay FROZEN.
       FIXED 2026-08-24, #91: renamed; 19 citing files updated, 2 frozen
         (migrations), 16 historical left as records (ADR-010 and this
         survey entry), 5 in closed artefacts under spike/ and infra/.

   F3  docs/_theme/index.html and index-de.html — RULING NEEDED
       current: the site's two landing PAGES live in the theme tree,
         beside css/, js/ and templates/
       candidates: stay in docs/_theme/ (they are hand-written HTML and
         reference the theme assets they sit with), or move to docs/
         (they are content, and docs/ is where content lives)
       evidence: copy_landing_pages() in build-docs.sh copies them

         called a defect here.
       RULED 2026-08-24, ADR-010, #90: they stay in docs/_theme/ — theme
         HTML lives with the theme, markdown content lives in docs/.
